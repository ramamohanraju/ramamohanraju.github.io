Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser. sitelatebanner uses them to keep the site working, to understand how it is used, and — only with your consent — to personalise advertising.

3. Your choices

Analytics and advertising cookies are set only after you select Accept All. Selecting Reject All leaves only the strictly necessary cookies in place and clears the rest. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie banner's Customise option.
