Fixture: Late-Loading Cookie Banner

Domain key: sitelatebanner

Purpose: A consent banner that is injected several seconds after the page has finished loading, the way a CMP delivered through a tag manager on a timer behaves. A consent scanner that only looks during the first few seconds of a page load reads this page as a site with no consent mechanism at all, and skips every check that depends on finding one.

Delay: 15 s by default. Override per load with ?delay=<ms>, e.g. ?delay=25000. instant.html is the same site with no delay — the control for a before/after comparison.

Welcome to SiteLateBanner

An otherwise compliant opt-in site: nothing beyond strictly necessary cookies is set until the visitor chooses, reject is honoured, and both policy links sit in the banner. The only thing wrong with it, from a scanner's point of view, is when the banner shows up.

What the scanner should find here

  • Banner present, with the markup and JavaScript API of a well-known hosted CMP (shape only — this is a local simulation, not that product)
  • Compliance type: opt-in; no analytics or advertising cookies before consent
  • Accept All sets _ga, _gid, _fbp, fr
  • Reject All sets none of them and clears any that exist
  • Banner comes back on a revisit without consent, and stays away once consent is stored
  • Cookie Policy, Privacy Policy and a preference centre are all reachable from the banner
